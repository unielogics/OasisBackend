# Adversarial coverage review of the "delivery" design (Dashboard + Backend)

I read cross, runtime, cc-domain, pay-domain and set-domain in full, and the cc-ui interaction and risk sections plus the pay-ui and set-ui risk sections. I checked claims against the live Squarespace and SMS Gate documentation (Sources at the end) and read-only host state.

## (A) Verdict

**The plan is strong on structure and weak at the seams.** The ports-and-adapters layout, the compiler/shim/harness approach, the ledger model and the milestone gates are sound. I would not start implementing from it as written.

I sampled 86 concrete items across the three screens and the cross-cutting contracts. 38 were covered with enough precision to implement. 48 were missing, underspecified, contradictory or based on a claim that does not hold.

**Three structural defects**
1. **Milestone dependencies are inverted.**
   - M3 (Settings) needs appointments, customers and vehicles. It uses them for closure and emergency affected counts, the idle banner, and VIP clients linked to customers. Those tables arrive in M4 step 1.
   - M3, M4 and M5 gates require the `design` seed profile. That profile is scheduled for M8.
2. **"Mechanical port, identical DOM" is contradicted in at least five places the design never lists as deviations.** These are `data-theme` on `<html>`, a single merged `globals.css`, href rewriting, template wiring changes (inputs and file upload that have no handlers in the designs), and money strings changing from whole dollars to cents.
   - The harness fails on unlisted differences. As specified, it fails on day one.
   - Only text and attribute replacement is supported. Row reordering, new elements and structural changes are not.
3. **Two Squarespace claims are wrong or unsound.**
   - The reference-token-in-a-URL-query-parameter matching scheme cannot work.
   - "Card brand and last4 from Transactions" does not hold. The documented field is a brand enum only.

**Verified external facts the design gets right or hedges adequately**
- Webhook Subscriptions are OAuth-only.
- API keys need Commerce Advanced.
- SMS Gate local mode needs HTTPS for non-127.0.0.1 webhook targets.
- Webhooks retry 14 times with exponential backoff, and the 2xx deadline is 30 s.
- The signature is HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body plus the timestamp.
